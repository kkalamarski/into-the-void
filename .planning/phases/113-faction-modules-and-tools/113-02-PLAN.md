Create `packages/items/src/definitions/faction-tools.ts` with all 40 faction tool definitions (4 factions x 2 tool lines x 5 rarities) using `getToolStats()` for all stat generation.

## Must-Haves (goal-backward)

1. 40 `ItemDefinition` exports: 10 Verdant, 10 Helix, 10 Nexus, 10 Unaffiliated
2. Every tool uses `getToolStats(toolType, rarity, tier)` -- zero hand-coded stat numbers
