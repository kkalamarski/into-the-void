Implement the accent detail drawing methods in ProceduralTileGenerator. These draw directly on the Phaser Graphics object before generateTexture() bakes it.

**Cube geometry constants (256x256 canvas):**
- Top diamond: vertices at (128, 0) top, (256, 64) right, (128, 128) bottom, (0, 64) left
- South face: from (0, 64) to (128, 128) to (128, 192) to (0, 128) — left trapezoid
- East face: from (128, 128) to (256, 64) to (256, 128) to (128, 192) — right trapezoid

**Use a seeded random per variant** to place detail elements. Use a simple hash: `variant * 7919 + charCodeAt(tileId, i)` for seeds.

**drawTopAccents() — surface features per biome:**

Natural biomes — recognizable shapes (per CONTEXT.md):
- `void_floor`: Small scattered pebbles (2-4px circles) and thin cracks (1px lines)
- `crystal_floor`: Reflective facet lines (thin bright lines at angles) and small crystal dots
- `toxic_floor`: Irregular stain patches (filled polygons in accent color, semi-transparent)
- `ruins_floor`: Grid-like crack pattern (perpendicular lines suggesting old tile floor)
- `ice_floor`: Long thin scratch lines and small frozen bubble dots
- `volcanic_floor`: Crack lines with orange glow accent and small ember dots
- `fungal_floor`: Circular spore dots and short curved tendril lines
- `crater_floor`: Tiny metallic fleck dots and radiating impact lines
- `tidal_floor`: Wavy sand ripple lines and small shell dots
- `kelp_floor`: Organic curved lines and small bubble circles
- `trench_floor`: Subtle pressure lines and rare bioluminescent dots
- `shore_transition`: Parallel wave lines and scattered pebble dots

Exotic biomes — abstract patterns (per CONTEXT.md):
- `void_rift_floor`: Glitchy pixel blocks (small rects at random positions), energy vein lines
- `crystalline_floor`: Prismatic facet lines in multiple accent colors, bright points
- `bioluminescent_floor`: Scattered glow dots (circles with slight size variation), organic vein lines

Wall/feature tiles — structural details:
- `void_wall`: Stacked horizontal layers with slight offset (stone strata)
- `crystal_formation`: Diagonal facet lines converging upward (crystal structure)
- `toxic_pool`: Bubble circles of varying size
- `ruins_wall`: Brick-pattern lines (horizontal + vertical offsets)
- `ice_wall`: Vertical crack lines and horizontal shelf lines
- `lava`: Flowing curved lines in yellow accent on orange
- `fungal_growth`: Large mushroom cap circles with stem lines
- `crater_debris`: Angular shard shapes (triangles)
- Other walls/features: Appropriate structural patterns

**drawSouthAccents() and drawEastAccents():**
These show cross-section / depth continuation:
- Use the same pattern style as top but adapted to the trapezoidal face geometry
- South face accents are slightly brighter (lit side)
- East face accents are dimmer (shadow side) — use `darkenColor(accent, 0.6)`
- Details should be sparser than top face (1/2 to 2/3 density)
- Clip all drawing to the face polygon bounds using graphics path + clip

**All accent drawing uses Phaser.Graphics primitives only:** `fillRect`, `fillCircle`, `lineTo/strokePath`, `fillTriangle`. No text, no images, no external dependencies.

**Variant differentiation:** Each variant uses different random positions for the same pattern type. Variant 0 (base) has standard density. Variant 1 (v2) shifts positions. Variant 2 (v3) uses slightly different accent color intensity.
