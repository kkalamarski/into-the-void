```markdown
# Faction Identity Design Document

> Single source of truth for faction gear identity. Referenced by all item definition files.
> Created: Phase 109 | Must be consulted before authoring any faction item.

## 1. Faction Stat Archetypes

### Verdant Dynamics
- **Primary archetype:** hazmat (resilience 30%, recovery 25%, durability 25%, vigor 20%)
- **Secondary archetype:** balanced (spread across all 8 stats)
- **Off-archetype:** combat (Verdant Security Division -- lore justification: Verdant maintains an armed security force to protect research installations and valuable biological specimens from Helix raids and wildlife; these units use combat gear because diplomacy doesn't work against silicon-armored predators)
- **Identity:** Environmental resilience and scientific versatility. Verdant suits keep you alive in hostile biomes; their defensive abilities let you outlast anything.

### Helix Extraction
- **Primary archetype:** tank (durability 35%, toughness 30%, resilience 15%, recovery 10%, vigor 10%)
- **Secondary archetype:** assault (power 35%, durability 25%, haste 25%, toughness 15%)
- **Off-archetype:** recon (Helix Deep Survey Teams -- lore justification: Helix deploys small recon teams into unexplored cavern systems and ruins to survey mineral deposits before committing heavy extraction equipment; these scouts need perception and speed, not armor)
- **Identity:** Raw toughness and industrial power. Helix suits absorb punishment and hit hard; they're built for the frontline of extraction operations.

### Nexus Frontiers
- **Primary archetype:** recon (perception 35%, haste 30%, vigor 25%, recovery 10%)
- **Secondary archetype:** scout (haste 30%, perception 25%, vigor 25%, recovery 10%, durability 10%)
- **Off-archetype:** assault (Nexus Enforcement Division -- lore justification: Nexus intelligence operations sometimes require force; their enforcement teams are equipped for rapid, decisive strikes when diplomacy and espionage have failed)
- **Identity:** Awareness and speed. Nexus suits see everything, react fastest, and are always one step ahead. Information advantage over brute force.

### Unaffiliated
- **Primary archetype:** scavenger (vigor 30%, recovery 25%, perception 25%, durability 12%, resilience 8%) -- NEW archetype, must be added to ARCHETYPE_PROFILES in utils.ts
- **Secondary archetype:** balanced (even spread)
- **Off-archetype:** hazmat (Wasteland Reclamation Crews -- lore justification: unaffiliated scavengers who specialize in recovering materials from toxic zones and contaminated ruins that the corporations have abandoned as unprofitable; they need environmental protection above all else)
- **Identity:** Jury-rigged survival. Unaffiliated gear cobbles together bits from every faction. Jack-of-all-trades combinations that no single faction offers.
```

- **Nexus tools:** Research/stealth specialization. toolType: 'research' (primary), 'stealth' (secondary). Precision instruments for intelligence gathering.
- **Unaffiliated tools:** Universal/combat specialization. toolType: 'universal' (primary), 'combat' (secondary). Multi-purpose improvised tools that do a bit of everything.
