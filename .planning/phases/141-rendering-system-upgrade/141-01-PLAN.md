Per CONTEXT.md decisions:
- **_wall** tiles: Darker top face already from palette. Add heavy vertical lines on south/east faces (bolted panels), thick border lines at top edges. Make them feel tall/imposing.
- **_door** tiles: Thin frame outline around top face edges. Small directional marks (arrow or gap indication). Floor-level — no side face accents needed.
- **_corridor** tiles: Grating lines across top face (parallel diagonal lines). Subtle edge detail on sides.
- **_decoration** tiles: Small raised rectangular detail on top face (console/machinery bump). Accent color dots for buttons/lights. Slight 3D feel from a small rectangle drawn slightly offset.
- **_accent** tiles: Scattered accent color dots/patches on top face (moss, glass panels, welding, grating holes).
- **_window** tiles: On south/east faces, draw a semi-transparent rectangle with accent color at ~0.3 alpha for the "glass panel" effect. Add a subtle glow line at panel edges.
- **_hazard** tiles: Diagonal caution stripes on top face (alternating accent and accentAlt colors). Glowing edge dots around perimeter.

The accent patterns are style-consistent across all 4 hubs — the faction identity comes from the color palette, not the pattern shape (per CONTEXT.md: "tile structures/shapes are similar across factions, color palettes make them distinct").
