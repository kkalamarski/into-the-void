<objective>
Fix entity sprite Y-positioning and depth sorting so all entity types (player, creatures, plants, minerals, NPCs) visually rest on tile ground surfaces and the local player sorts correctly relative to nearby tall sprites.

Purpose: Entity sprites currently appear to sink below the tile surface because their containers are placed at the tile diamond center (y=0 from gridToScreen), but the visual ground surface of the isometric cube tiles is 64px lower (the bottom half of the diamond face). Adding a +64px Y-offset to all entity containers shifts sprite feet to the tile surface. The local player's depth boost also needs unification between WorldScene (0.1) and DepthSorter (0.001) to prevent inconsistent Z-order flickering.

Output: EntityRenderer.ts, WorldScene.ts, and DepthSorter.ts with corrected Y-positioning and unified depth boost
</objective>
